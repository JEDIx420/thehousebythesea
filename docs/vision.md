# Creative vision — The House by the Sea

## Elevator pitch
A young man who spent his childhood dreaming of the ocean finally owns a modest boat. He fishes, takes practical jobs, explores a lively chain of twelve islands, falls in love and saves money to build a small, beautiful beach home they can share.

## Six pillars
1. **The sea is freedom:** the boat feels pleasant, controllable and important; walking off it is part of the same game, not a separate minigame.
2. **Places have personality:** a fixed twelve-island map with paths, harbours, forests, shops, homes, landmarks, recurring people and reasons to return.
3. **Work feels worthwhile:** fishing, deliveries, trade and helpful errands provide satisfying but ungrindy financial progress.
4. **Romance is mutual:** the woman is a full character with interests, work and choices. Boat outings, conversations and shared memories build an authentic relationship.
5. **One personal home:** earn a plot, hire builders, fund construction and furnish a modest seaside cottage—not a village/base builder.
6. **Enjoy ordinary moments:** golden-hour sailing, birds, waves, markets, festivals, night lamps, sunsets and quiet beaches. No compulsory violence or punishment.

## Experience / art
Single-player third-person 3D, cosy and colourful, approachable keyboard/mouse and touchpad controls, soft stylised visuals rather than hyperrealism. The player should always have a nearby meaningful activity. Islands are authored once, even if procedural tools help shape them; they are not randomly invented during each play session.

## Story promise
A brief optional childhood prologue shows the boy dreaming of a boat; the adult game begins after he has bought it. The protagonist works and explores, courts a woman he knows or meets in town, discovers potential house sites, and eventually builds a home together. The archipelago remains explorable after the main arc.

## Not this game
No endless procedurally spawning ocean, world-saving fog conspiracy, open warfare, survival hunger loops, village building, paid services, backend, multiplayer or Cloudflare. The long-term goal is twelve islands, not twelve islands in the first playable.

## Stage boundary
Stage 0: prove the technical ingredients. Stage 1: a compelling 10–15 minute First Voyage on two islands. The creator decides whether the feel is right before authorising any larger world build. See docs/first-voyage.md for precise gate.
