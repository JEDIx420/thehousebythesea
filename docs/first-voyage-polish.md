## 2. Make walking feel expressive, not rigid
- Introduce transient world-space velocity and an explicit idle/start/walk/stop controller. Tune via actual browser play; suggested starting ranges: reach max walk speed within ~100–180ms, stop within ~80–150ms, brisk walking speed around existing 5.2 units/s adjusted after map sizing; preserve input responsiveness, normalize diagonals, avoid ice-skating or delayed release. Use dt-capped fixed/substep collision, clear velocity on modal/blur/pause/fishing/board/recover/reload. No drift when input released or on collision.
- Face actual travel/intended direction with hysteresis, avoid flickering at diagonal boundaries; prefer 8 distinct authored directions, at least four high-quality directions plus tested diagonals if 8 proves inconsistent. Create authored planted-foot walk loop (8–12 frames preferred), natural hip/shoulder weight shift, arm swings, subtle head/torso bob and start/stop/idle blends via pose transitions (no blurry alpha ghosting). Use locomotion phase derived from traveled distance/speed, not global elapsed; blocked character must not moonwalk. Each NPC independent cycle, idle blinking/breathing/occasional small gestures; authored seated and fishing poses preserve anchors. Move at correct pixel density. No merely speeding up existing six-frame loop.
- Preserve projected screen-relative inputs and collision, implement smooth corner sliding only when valid. Maintain logical fractional positions and pixel-perfect render snapping with consistent camera/sprite rounding; measure at 60 and 30 Hz. Camera critically damped or time-constant follow, small look-ahead, dead zone, sensible bounds; comfortable default zoom and unobstructed avatar; no large abrupt shifts on diagonal motion/zoom/dock. Respect reduced-motion preference. Do not use interpolation to hide collision errors.
- Boat feel already has acceleration; preserve actual boarding, directional boat frames, hull checks, fishing and companion movement. Refine boat↔foot transition and step onto pier, short animation that never moves through forbidden space. Add soft footsteps on wood/sand/grass and short interact/fishing/catch SFX.
- NPCs: replace near-static tiny loops with varied authored paths, pauses and purpose (harbourmaster checking cargo, fish seller at stall, Mira sketching or looking out, Saltglass fisher tending nets). Ensure accessible interaction windows, stable quest targets, valid routes and no crowded choke points. Do not introduce complex scheduling or block story progression.
