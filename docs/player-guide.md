# First Voyage — creator playtest

This is the Stage 0 + Stage 1 approval build. Later islands, house construction and further relationship chapters are locked pending your approval.

## Open and begin

`npm ci`, `npm run build`, `npm run preview -- --port 4173`, then open http://localhost:4173/thehousebythesea/ . The build is fully static. No account or service is required. Use a WebGL2 desktop browser with hardware acceleration. The optional childhood memory can be read, skipped, or closed with Escape.

## Controls

- W / Up moves forward; S / Down walks backward or slows/reverses the boat.
- A / Left and D / Right turn Rowan or steer the boat. These are third-person turning controls; movement follows his facing direction.
- Drag the view to orbit the camera independently.
- E talks to the nearest person, boards near your boat, or ties up near a pier.
- F casts from a pier or the boat; wait for **A bite!**, then Space reels in. F cancels without loss.
- M opens the chart; J opens your journal; Escape pauses or closes a menu.
- Pause contains audio controls, save, credits and recovery to the current island dock.

## Suggested 10–15 minute route

1. Read or skip Rowan's childhood memory. Look around Havenport; the red-shirted woman is Mira, the blue-shirted man at the pier is Ellis, and the green-shirted man is Tomas, the fish seller.
2. Walk to Ellis, press E and accept the net-needle parcel. Walk along the stone waterfront path to Mira and speak with her. Invite her to sketch Saltglass if you like; deferring is fine.
3. Walk out onto the wooden pier. E boards the boat. The bow points north initially. Hold W, steer gently into the open channel on the east side of the islands, and head north toward Saltglass, about 220 metres away. The objective shows your distance.
4. Approach the east pier at Saltglass; E ties up and places you on its verified walk surface. Walk inland to Nessa near the pier, E, and hand over the parcel for 120 coins. Reopening her conversation never pays again.
5. On the pier, F casts. Space after the bite puts a fish in your creel. Try a second cast for the other species. Cancel a cast with F, or let one escape; there is no penalty. Nessa or Tomas buys your catch. A delivery plus both fish takes 25 starting coins to 195.
6. Follow the path northwest inland to the shell arch and take in the view. If Mira came along, her sketch is part of the shared afternoon. Reboard at the pier and sail south to Havenport.
7. Speak to Mira again to remember the crossing. Visit the notice by the north beach (near the end of the waterfront path) to see the home fund. The first milestone is 150 coins; 500 is an early aspiration, not a construction purchase in this build.
8. Open journal, chart, settings and credits. Save, refresh, continue: your coins, parcel, fish inventory, discoveries, shared memory, player and boat positions should remain.

Take your time. The automated route is deliberately faster than a person reading, exploring and learning the controls. Feedback on the pacing, art, camera and handling is the purpose of this gate.
