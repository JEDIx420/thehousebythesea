# Stage 1B art direction — creator changes requested

Issue #4 governs this reset. Stage 1 is NOT visually approved. No Stage 2.

## Target
A sun-warmed working Mediterranean coast: white limewashed buildings, faded teal doors, terracotta roofs, weathered honey timber, linen clothes, pine/sage foliage and clear turquoise shallows leading into deep blue sea. Detailed stylised Polyfork mesh silhouettes, human proportions and useful harbour clutter. Avoid block/voxel people, flat plank wakes, oversized signs, random prop scatter, overexposed pale terrain and catalogue scenes disconnected from gameplay.

## Composition and scale
Humans approximately1.75m; working boats6–8m, doors2m, piers wide enough for safe boarding. Rowan wears a muted work outfit, Mira warm ochre/coral with distinct hair/headwear; fishermen darker waterproof clothing. Characters must be actual rigged/inspected models with genuine animation or an honestly documented legal motion fallback. Keep a coherent faceting/material family rather than mixing realistic and toy assets.

Havenport: a sheltered busy quay, cream shopfronts, terracotta roof rhythm, café tables/planters, neatly moored boats and nets/crates forming small activity clusters. Saltglass: quieter green-blue fishing hamlet, beach grass, rock headland and shell arch. Two islands only; authored paths and NPC/boat checkpoints remain stable.

## Light, water and camera
Warm low directional sun with moderate exposure, cooler sky fill, saturated midtones and legible dark values. A/B existing lighting before choosing; no bleaching the sand and buildings. Water uses coherent small moving wave bands, shoreline/depth colour and soft irregular foam, never tiled rectangular wake cards. Hills should feel sculpted with sand/grass/rock transitions; exact rendered triangle surface remains the collision ground truth.

Follow camera near human eye height: about8–10m behind on foot,12–15m aboard, lower pitch. Ground/structure clearance and orbit safety must keep the player readable. Character closeup, harbour approach, sailing and landing compositions must be captured from controllable gameplay.

## Reference sources and V0 audition
Use the actual licensed Polyfork Coast & Boardwalk and Mediterranean Island Village GLBs described in Issue #4; inspect local downloads and their material/animation/scale properties in the browser showroom. Existing Holidaymaker and Small Fishing Boat are candidates, not automatically approved. Audition Mediterranean caique before choosing the playable vessel. Catalogue thumbnails are sourcing aids only and are not visual acceptance evidence.

## Staged gate
V0: actual GLB showroom + an integrated harbour/boat checkpoint. V1: people and vessel. V2: terrain/water/light/camera. V3: settlements and life. V4: six matching before/after views,30–60s actual play, independent art/code reviews, regression/CI and creator approval. Passing tests cannot approve art.
