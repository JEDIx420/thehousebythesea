Add a broader trading/fishing/job economy, introduce the choice of three distinct potential house plots, and make travel feel like an inhabited regional network.

## Work plan
- Begin by implementing accepted Stage 1 feedback and locking baseline save schema compatibility. Benchmark performance before adding new terrain.
- Choose four islands from Willowmere, Bellport, Orchard Point, Gullwatch or creator-approved alternates; plan each with a memorable landmark, usable dock, walkable routes, recurring NPC, paid job and a reason to return.
- Use the authoring/export pipeline proved in Stage 0; add streaming and distant silhouettes, ensuring all six island crossings work without a blank scene or lost boat.
- Expand fishing varieties, deliveries/market prices and inventory as needed; keep work rewarding, currency simple and quest payouts idempotent.
- Author three potential **home plots** with distinctive social/scenic advantages. Allow the player to compare them; do not build the whole house or rush the relationship climax.

- Verify save migrations, all six shore transitions, performance on approaching a busy village, credits and new assets. Independent reviewer checks in batches and creator signs off on the area.

## Exit
